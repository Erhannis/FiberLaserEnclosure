FreeCAD sheet metal fiber laser enclosure, with slots for 5.5"x2.5"x0.125" laser windows (with foam padding).
WARNING: Untested, as of yet.  Also, I'm not certified for laser enclosures or nothin'; I just made a box.  Use your judgement of its safety, not mine.  I disclaim responsibility.

Note you need the Sheet Metal workbench add-on.
When you unfold the parts to export to svg, be sure to set the manual k factor to 0.5 or the measurements might be sliiightly off.  (But probably only like, 0.1mm or st.)
